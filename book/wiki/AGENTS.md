# Wiki Agents

This folder is for agent-maintained Markdown wikis: lightweight knowledge bases that help connect concepts, literatures, evidence, mechanisms, rival explanations, and manuscript sections.

## Start Here

- Read `book/wiki/_codex/wiki_workflow.md` before creating, editing, or reorganizing wiki pages.
- For citation and source rules, also read `book/_codex/library.md`.

## Local Rule

- Wiki topic folders live directly under `book/wiki/`, numbered with four-digit prefixes in the order their themes first become important in the manuscript.
- Do not create a separate `topics/` wrapper folder.
- Do not create separate source-ledger pages such as `sources.md`. Cite or link sources inline where the claim, mechanism, alternative explanation, or open question appears.
- Prefer links to other wiki pages, source notes, PDFs, chapter sections, and quote banks rather than bare paths.
